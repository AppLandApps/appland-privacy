# Privacy Policy for Subwhere London

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Subwhere London ("Subwhere LDN" on your Home Screen; "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App and its widgets on iPhone and iPad.

Subwhere London is published by AppLand (Stefan Almqvist), based in Sweden.

Contact: <applandapps@gmail.com>

## Short version

The App does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your location and your camera image are used on your device and are never sent to us. To show live trains, the App downloads Tube data through a small service we run. Those requests contain no account, no identifier and no location coordinates, and we keep no record of them.

## What the App uses on your device

**Camera feed.** Used by Apple's ARKit to show the augmented reality view and to recognise the buildings around you, so the trains line up with the street. The camera image is processed on your device and is never recorded, saved or transmitted.

**Location.** Used to place trains relative to where you are standing, to find your nearest station and point the way to it, and to choose the station the widget shows. Your location is processed on your device. It is never sent to us, never stored as a location history, and never shared or sold.

**Device motion and compass heading.** Used for the AR view and the station compass. Processed on your device only.

**Data kept on your device.** So the widget can work without opening the App, the App keeps the list of Tube stations, the latest departures for your nearest station, TfL timetables for recent nearest and pinned stations, and the latest line status. It also remembers whether you have seen the introduction. None of this is sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the App.

We do not use analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising, crash-reporting services or any other third-party data collection. The App does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Widgets

Subwhere London offers optional Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that show upcoming departures. A widget shows either a station you choose or, by default, the station nearest you. To find the nearest station it reads your device's location on your device, including while the App is closed. As in the App, your location is used only on your device to work out which station is nearest. It is never stored as a history, sent to us, shared or sold.

## Live train data

Train positions, departures, timetables and line status come from Transport for London's open data. The App does not contact TfL itself. It asks a small service that AppLand runs on Cloudflare, which fetches the data from TfL and passes it on. This keeps our TfL access key out of the App and the load on TfL low.

These requests contain no account, no device identifier and no location coordinates. Most ask for the trains on the whole network or the status of every line, which is the same for everyone. When the App or a widget needs departures or a timetable for a single station, the request names that station and nothing else about you. For the nearest-station badge and the widget's default mode, that is the station nearest you.

Like any internet connection, a request reaches our service with your device's IP address, which is needed to send the answer back. We do not keep logs of requests or IP addresses, and we do not use them to identify or follow anyone. Cloudflare processes this connection data on our behalf to deliver and protect the service, under its privacy policy: <https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>

TfL receives requests only from our service, never from your device, so it does not see your IP address.

## Apple services

The AR view uses Apple's ARKit location anchors (geotracking). To line up the scene, ARKit downloads Apple Maps localisation data for the area you are in and matches it against the camera image on your device. Apple states that no camera images are sent to Apple. The live map is Apple Maps (MapKit).

Apple may collect data through these services, the App Store and TestFlight as described in its privacy policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you test a beta version through TestFlight, Apple shares some information with us: crash logs and usage information from the beta, your name and email address if we invited you by email (not if you joined through a public link), and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. We use it only to test and improve the App, and we do not share it with anyone. Apple explains TestFlight's data handling here: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/>

## Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply. We delete them on request.

## Permissions

The App will ask for permission to use:

**Camera** - needed for the AR view.

**Location (while using the App or its widgets)** - needed to place trains around you, to find your nearest station, and for the widget's nearest-station mode.

You can change these at any time in iOS Settings under Subwhere LDN.
