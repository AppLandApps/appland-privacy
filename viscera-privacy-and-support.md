# VISCERA: Privacy Policy and Support

App: VISCERA

Last updated: 2026-07-19

## Privacy Policy

### Short version

VISCERA does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything you make stays on your device.

### What we collect

Nothing. VISCERA does not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or location. There are no servers behind the app, and it does not send your data anywhere.

### Microphone

VISCERA can record sound through your device microphone so you can use your own voice or surroundings as source material. Microphone access is used only while you are actively recording, and only after you grant permission. Recorded audio is written to local storage on your device and is never uploaded or shared by the app. You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time in the iOS Settings app, in which case recording features are simply unavailable.

### Your audio and presets

Any sounds you record, patches you save, and audio you export stay on your device, and in your own iCloud or Files backups if you choose to save them there. VISCERA has no server access and cannot see or retrieve this content.

### Purchases

VISCERA offers a single one-time Pro Unlock in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. VISCERA never sees or stores your payment information. Apple handling of your purchase is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

### No tracking or advertising

VISCERA contains no advertising, no analytics or measurement SDKs, and no third-party trackers. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not request permission to track you across apps or websites.

### Children

VISCERA does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is not directed at children under 13, and it produces loud, intense audio. Headphones and adult discretion are recommended.

### Data retention and deletion

All of your content lives only on your device, so you are in full control of it. Deleting a recording or preset in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes all of its local data.

### Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple, which distributes the app and processes in-app purchases. VISCERA integrates no other third-party services.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted at this URL with a new Last updated date. Material changes are also noted in the app release notes.

## Support and FAQ

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature request? Email applandapps@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version so we can help faster. We aim to reply within a few business days.

### I do not hear anything

Check these in order:
1. Turn up the volume and make sure the silent switch is not muting the device.
2. Load or record a sound first. VISCERA plays back a sound you provide, so with nothing loaded there is little to hear.
3. Touch and move on the pad. Sound is generated as you play.
4. If you are recording, confirm you granted microphone permission in Settings, VISCERA, Microphone.

### How do I get a sound into the app?

Record one with the microphone using the record control, then play it back on the pad. You can also import audio by tapping the X axis boxes at the top.

### How do I record my own voice?

Grant microphone permission when prompted, or later in Settings, VISCERA, Microphone. Tap record, make your sound, and stop. It becomes the source you play and transform.

### How do I export what I make?

When stopping a recording session you are automatically promted to save your output to a 24-bit WAV file.

### What does Pro Unlock include?

Pro Unlock is a single one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks all audio slots, audio import, audio export and the save presets function. Once purchased it is permanently tied to your Apple ID.

### I bought Pro but it is not showing, or I got a new phone

Use Restore Purchases, available on the paywall or in Settings. This re-checks your Apple ID with the App Store and restores Pro Unlock at no charge. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase.

### Does VISCERA work offline?

Yes. VISCERA runs entirely on the device and needs no internet connection. A connection is only used by Apple to process or restore a purchase.

### Does VISCERA collect my data or send my recordings anywhere?

No. See the Privacy Policy above. Nothing is collected, and your audio never leaves your device unless you choose to export and share it yourself.

### It is very loud

VISCERA is built to produce intense sound. Start at a low volume, especially on headphones, and raise it gradually.

### Bug reports and feature requests

Email applandapps@gmail.com with a short description, your device model, and your iOS version. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.
