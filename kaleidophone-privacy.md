# Kaleidophone — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 October 2026*

Kaleidophone is made by Stefan Almqvist (AppLand). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **Kaleidophone does not collect, store or share any personal data.**

## Camera

Kaleidophone uses your camera to turn light and motion into sound. Camera images are processed live on your device and immediately discarded. They are never saved, recorded or sent anywhere.

## Recordings

When you record, the audio is saved as a WAV file on your device. Recordings only leave your device if you choose to share or export them yourself, using the iOS share sheet.

## No data collection

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No data is sent to AppLand or any third party

## Children

Kaleidophone does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
